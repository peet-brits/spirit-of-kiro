# Spirit of Kiro — Product Overview

Spirit of Kiro is an AI-powered infinite crafting workshop game, built as a demo project to showcase best practices for Kiro and AI-assisted engineering.

## Core Game Loop

1. **Dispense** — Pull an item from the dispenser. Items are either AI-generated (unique name, description, damage, quirks) or recycled from the shared discarded pool.
2. **Craft** — Use item skills on other items at the workbench. Skills are open-ended: cut, smash, weld, enchant, install, etc. AI determines the outcome of every crafting action.
3. **Sell** — Take an item to the appraiser. AI appraises and sells it, giving the player a cut.
4. **Repeat** — Use proceeds to buy discarded items or keep crafting.

## Key Design Principles

- **AI enables emergent gameplay**: There is no hardcoded crafting schema. Any item can interact with any other item; the AI determines the result.
- **Scale improves efficiency**: Discarded items are pooled and reused rather than deleted. The more players, the fewer brand-new AI generations are needed (lower cost, faster response).
- **Vector image deduplication**: Item images are generated by AI (Nova Canvas) but reused when a similar description already has a matching image in the vector database, reducing cost and latency over time.

## Authentication

Users sign up and sign in via Amazon Cognito. All gameplay actions require an authenticated WebSocket session.
